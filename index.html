FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you send a transport enquiry.

How do I book a vehicle?

Fill in the booking form, select your vehicle and pickup date, then click Book Now on WhatsApp.

Can I choose the pickup date?

Yes. The selected date is included automatically in the WhatsApp message sent to the owner.

How is the freight confirmed?

Vehicle availability and final freight are discussed and confirmed directly with the transport team.

Which vehicles are available?

The website currently displays Tata Pickup, 14 Feet, 17 Feet, 19 Feet and 22 Feet options, subject to availability.

Do you handle business and industrial cargo?

You can send an enquiry for commercial stock, machinery, construction material and other business goods.
